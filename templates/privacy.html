YOUR TRUST MATTERS

Privacy
Policy.

This Privacy Policy explains how Lucente handles personal information when you visit our website, contact us or use our fitness coaching services. We aim to treat your information respectfully, use it only for appropriate purposes and explain the choices available to you.

Please read: This policy must be checked against the actual tools, data practices and business arrangements used by Lucente before launch. It does not claim that information is collected, stored, shared or deleted in a particular way unless that practice has been implemented.

01. Who This Policy Covers

This policy applies to people who visit the Lucente website, enquire about coaching, communicate with Lucente or participate in services offered by Lucente.

In this policy, “Lucente”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to the business or individual operating the Lucente website and coaching services. Before public launch, the identity and contact details of the responsible business should be provided here.

This policy should be read alongside the Terms & Conditions and any additional privacy notice provided when you use a particular service or third-party platform.

02. Information We May Collect

The information collected depends on how you interact with Lucente and which services you choose to use. It may include:

  • Contact information: such as your name, phone number, email address or contact details you provide.
  • Enquiry information: such as questions, messages, preferred services and the information you choose to include in an enquiry.
  • Coaching information: such as your fitness goals, training experience, exercise preferences, available equipment and progress updates.
  • Health-related information: information you voluntarily share about injuries, limitations, allergies or other factors relevant to exercise guidance.
  • Payment information: records needed to confirm a purchase, such as the service purchased, amount, date and transaction reference. Payment providers may process card or account details through their own systems.
  • Communications: messages and information exchanged with Lucente through the contact channels you use.
  • Technical information: limited information that may be processed by the website host or your browser, such as access logs, device information or IP address, depending on the hosting configuration.

We do not ask you to send information that is unnecessary for your enquiry or coaching. Please avoid sending passwords, payment security codes or other sensitive credentials through messages.

03. How We Use Information

Depending on the interaction, Lucente may use information for purposes such as:

  • Responding to enquiries and explaining available services.
  • Setting up and delivering the coaching service you purchase.
  • Preparing or adjusting training guidance based on information relevant to your stated goals and limitations.
  • Reviewing progress and communicating about check-ins.
  • Confirming payments, maintaining appropriate business records and addressing billing questions.
  • Protecting the website, preventing misuse and troubleshooting technical issues.
  • Meeting legal, regulatory or legitimate record-keeping obligations where applicable.
  • Handling complaints, resolving disputes and protecting legitimate rights.

We should not use personal information for an unrelated purpose that is incompatible with the reason it was collected without an appropriate lawful basis or any consent required by law.

04. Health and Fitness Information

Fitness coaching may involve information about your exercise history, injuries, physical limitations, allergies or other health-related circumstances. This information deserves careful handling because it may be personal and sensitive.

Please share only information relevant to your coaching needs. We may use information you provide to understand your stated limitations and determine whether the service can reasonably support your goals. Coaching is not a substitute for medical assessment or treatment.

Where applicable law requires consent or another specific legal basis for handling health-related information, Lucente should obtain or establish that basis before processing it.

Do not send medical records or detailed health documents unless they are genuinely needed and you have been told how they will be handled.

05. Legal Grounds for Processing

Depending on the law that applies and the nature of the activity, personal information may be processed where necessary to respond to your request, deliver an agreed service, comply with a legal obligation, protect legitimate interests where permitted, or on the basis of valid consent.

If we rely on consent for a particular purpose, you may be able to withdraw that consent. Withdrawal does not necessarily make earlier lawful processing invalid, and certain information may still need to be retained where lawfully required.

The appropriate legal basis depends on the actual processing activity. This section should be reviewed against Lucente's operations and applicable privacy laws before publication.

06. WhatsApp and Other Communication Platforms

If you choose to contact Lucente through WhatsApp or another external platform, your communication will also be subject to that platform's terms, privacy policy and technical practices.

The platform may process account information, messages, device data and other information according to its own policies. Lucente does not control every aspect of an external platform's data handling.

Use a communication method you are comfortable with. Avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information in messages, and ask about an alternative channel if you do not wish to share particular information through a third-party service.

07. Payments and Third-Party Providers

If you purchase a service, a payment provider or financial institution may process information needed to complete the transaction, prevent fraud or meet legal requirements.

The information processed by a provider depends on the payment method and provider you use. Its own privacy terms apply to processing it controls.

Lucente should not collect or store full card details, passwords or security codes unless there is a lawful, necessary and properly secured arrangement for doing so. Use reputable payment providers and review their terms before integrating them into the website.

08. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small data files that websites may place on a device. The current Lucente website should be checked to determine whether it uses cookies, analytics, advertising pixels or other tracking technologies.

Essential technologies may be used to provide a requested website function or maintain security. Non-essential analytics or advertising technologies should be used only with any consent or notice required by applicable law.

Your browser may offer controls to view, block or delete cookies. Blocking certain cookies may affect website functionality. This policy should be updated if analytics or advertising tools are added in the future.

09. When Information May Be Shared

Lucente does not need unrestricted access to your personal information. Information should be shared only where reasonably necessary for a stated purpose and where legally permitted.

Depending on the service, sharing may occur with:

  • Website hosting or technical service providers that support website operation.
  • Payment providers or financial institutions involved in a transaction.
  • Communication providers you choose to contact Lucente through.
  • Coaches or authorised personnel who need relevant information to provide the agreed service.
  • Professional advisers or authorities where disclosure is legally required or otherwise lawfully justified.

Before adding any provider, Lucente should review what information it receives, how it uses that information and what contractual or security protections are appropriate.

We should not sell personal information or disclose identifiable client health details for unrelated marketing purposes.

10. International Processing

Some website, messaging, hosting or payment providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where this occurs, applicable law may require particular safeguards, notices or transfer arrangements.

Lucente should identify the relevant providers and assess their processing locations before making specific promises about where information is stored or whether it leaves a particular country.

11. Data Security

Lucente should use reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the information it handles. Depending on the actual systems used, good practices may include limiting access, protecting accounts with strong authentication, keeping software updated, using secure connections and avoiding unnecessary copies of personal information.

No website, messaging platform or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We should not claim that information is encrypted, backed up or protected by a particular security system unless that protection is actually in place.

If a security incident occurs, Lucente will take appropriate steps in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

12. How Long Information Is Retained

Information should be kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to legal obligations, legitimate dispute needs and other applicable requirements.

Different records may require different retention periods. For example, enquiry messages, coaching notes and payment records may not all need to be retained for the same length of time.

Lucente should establish a practical retention schedule and securely delete or anonymise information when it is no longer needed and no lawful reason requires it to be kept.

13. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on the law applicable to you, you may have rights concerning your personal information, which may include:

  • Requesting information about the personal data being processed.
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Requesting deletion where applicable and legally available.
  • Withdrawing consent for processing based on consent.
  • Objecting to or restricting certain processing where the law provides that right.
  • Raising a complaint with the relevant privacy or data protection authority.

These rights are not identical in every jurisdiction and may be subject to legal exceptions. To make a request, contact Lucente through the verified contact details published on the website.

We may need to verify your identity before responding to a request to prevent disclosure to the wrong person. We will handle requests within the period required by applicable law.

14. Children's Privacy

Lucente recognises the importance of protecting information relating to children and teenagers. Age-related consent and processing requirements vary depending on the applicable law and the service being offered.

Where required, Lucente will obtain appropriate parent or guardian consent and follow any additional safeguards before processing a minor's personal information.

A parent or guardian who has a concern about information supplied by a child may contact Lucente through its official contact channel. Any request will be assessed under applicable law.

15. Marketing and Testimonials

Lucente may wish to share business updates or information about its services. Where marketing consent or an opt-out is required, the applicable process will be provided.

Coaching participation does not itself grant permission to use your name, photograph, testimonial, body measurements or progress records in public marketing. Lucente should obtain separate permission where required and explain the intended use.

You may decline a request to use identifiable content for marketing without that decision alone affecting your coaching. If you previously gave permission and wish to withdraw it, contact Lucente. Withdrawal may not affect uses already lawfully completed, but future use will be handled according to the permission and applicable law.

16. External Links

The Lucente website may contain links to other websites or services. Those external services operate under their own terms and privacy practices.

Lucente is not responsible for the content or privacy practices of a website it does not control. Review the relevant policy before submitting personal information to another service.

17. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Lucente's services, technology, providers or legal obligations change.

The latest version should be published on this page with an accurate last-updated date. Where applicable law requires notice or consent for a material change, Lucente should follow that requirement.

18. Privacy Questions and Complaints

For questions about this policy, requests relating to personal information or privacy complaints, contact Lucente through the official contact method provided on the website.

Before launch, replace this instruction with a verified, monitored email address or other appropriate contact method. Where required by applicable law, identify the responsible business and the relevant grievance or privacy contact.

19. Additional Information for India

Where applicable, Lucente will handle personal information consistently with relevant Indian privacy and consumer laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and any provisions and rules that are in force and apply to the particular processing activity.

The specific legal obligations depend on the applicable law, commencement of relevant provisions, the nature of the business and the processing involved. This policy is not a substitute for checking those requirements before launch.
